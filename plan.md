# River — implementation plan

A modern, Tron-styled take on River Raid: an endless neon river, a tilted chase camera, and desktop only.
The working title is "River"; the final name is still open.

Live build: https://riverride.surge.sh

## Done

- **Milestone 1: flight.** Seeded endless river (`src/world/river.ts`) with smooth banks, islands and bridge necks; streamed terrain chunks; Tron materials with bloom; ship flight with speed control and banking; terrain crashes.
- **Milestone 2: core loop.** Enemies (ships, helicopters, jets), fuel depots, bridges as checkpoints, shooting, fuel drain and refuel, score, lives, extra lives, game over. Includes the HUD and explosions.
- **Milestone 3: audio.** Everything lives in `src/audio/`; the sound designer's guide is `audio-src/README.md`.
  - Web Audio engine with sfx, ui and music buses, a limiter, and unlock on the first key press.
  - Sounds are found by file name, with random variations, pitch jitter, voice limits and cooldowns.
  - State-driven loops: engine layers crossfaded by speed, refuel, low-fuel alarm, per-jet doppler, helicopter rotor.
  - Panning and distance attenuation, and music ducking on big moments.
  - Adaptive music: synced stems layered by sector, a tension layer on low fuel, muffled while crashed.
  - Synthesised placeholders for every SFX until real files arrive.
  - Dev audio panel (`): live sliders, soundboard, file reload, and Save straight into `src/audio/sounds.ts`.
  - The build encodes sources to Opus and AAC with ffmpeg (`scripts/convert-audio.mjs`).

- **Milestone 4: look and feel.**
  - World: a horizon wall where the world fades out (gradient sky, grid, stars, glow line). Reflective water (mirrored pass with ripples) and wakes behind moving boats. Per-sector colour themes that blend across bridges (`sectorTheme` in `src/world/props.ts`). Deterministic bank props: towers, pylon runs with cables, blocks, beacons. Bridges get chasing road lamps and the next sector number painted on the deck.
  - Player and entities: ship canopy, an engine flame that scales with speed, and a muzzle flash. Bullets are glowing streaks. Movers flash a ring and swell when they wake up, and incoming jets get an edge-of-screen warning. Kills throw wireframe debris. Bridges split at the middle, swing down off their hinges, and sink.
  - Feel: FOV and camera distance follow speed, with speed lines at full throttle. Hit-stop on crashes and bridge kills, and slow motion through the final crash (`src/game/timewarp.ts`). Trauma-based shake with a chromatic-aberration kick. Score pop-ups. Fade-out at the end of a crash, a swoop-in on respawn, and a wreck camera on game over.
  - Post: one finishing pass for vignette, grain, optional scanlines (C), aberration and fades. Quality presets low/medium/high (Q) set pixel ratio, bloom size, particle counts and reflection resolution. Both settings are saved to `localStorage`.

## Architecture rules (keep these)

- Game logic in `src/game` and `src/world` is pure TypeScript with no Three.js and no DOM. It runs on a fixed 60 Hz step and stays unit-testable.
- The game talks outward only through events (`game.drainEvents()`) and readable state. The renderer, HUD and audio all consume the same stream.
- All tuning numbers live in `src/game/tuning.ts`.
- Everything is deterministic from the seed. Sectors are generated by `spawnSection(river, section)`.

## Milestone 5: Gameplay depth

These are open for discussion. Pick what fits once audio and visuals feel good.

- [ ] Ground enemies on the banks: tanks or turrets that fire at the player (needs enemy projectiles).
- [ ] Balloons or mines that drift in the river.
- [ ] Destructible terrain props on the banks for bonus points.
- [ ] Scoring: combo multiplier for quick consecutive kills, a bonus for clearing a sector without refuelling, and a bonus for leaving fuel depots unshot.
- [ ] Difficulty curve tuning by playtesting. Track average sector reached and deaths by cause.
- [ ] Daily seed mode: same river for everyone each day, shown in the HUD.

## Milestone 6: Polish and shipping

### 6.1 Menus and flow

- [ ] Title screen: logo, the river flying by in the background as attract mode, "Press Space", and title music.
- [ ] UI sounds for menus (move, select, back).
- [ ] Pause with Esc or P, with a pause menu (resume, settings, quit to title). Pause automatically when the tab loses focus.
- [ ] Game-over screen with stats (sector, kills by type, accuracy, distance) and a local top-10 table with initials.

### 6.2 Settings (saved to `localStorage`)

- [ ] Volume sliders: master, music, SFX (M already mutes).
- [ ] Graphics quality preset, and a toggle for screen shake and flashing effects (accessibility).
- [ ] Key rebinding.

### 6.3 Input

- [ ] Gamepad support (Gamepad API): left stick or D-pad to steer and set speed, A or RT to fire. Show prompts for the active device.
- [ ] Merge keyboard and gamepad into the single `InputState` the game already uses.

### 6.4 Performance and build

- [ ] Split three.js into its own chunk (silences the 600 kB warning and helps caching).
- [ ] Use instanced meshes for entities if counts grow. Profile on a mid-range laptop GPU.
- [ ] Add `npm run deploy` (build, then `surge dist riverride.surge.sh`). Optionally, a GitHub Action to deploy on push to `master`.
- [ ] Favicon, page title with the final name, and Open Graph image and meta tags.
